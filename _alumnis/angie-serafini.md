**Angie Serafini: Three Generations, One KCC Connection**

Kankakee Community College has been part of Angie Serafini’s family for three generations.

“KCC has been a part of my family’s story for decades,” Angie said. Both of her parents are KCC graduates. Angie earned an Associate in Arts transfer degree from KCC in 2001, and years later, her sons attended KCC.

“Seeing multiple generations of my family benefit from the opportunities KCC provides has only deepened my appreciation for the impact it has on our community,” she said.

For Angie, attending KCC was the start of an educational path she had not anticipated.

“At the time, I never imagined KCC would be the beginning of an educational journey that would continue for many years,” Angie said. “It gave me the foundation and confidence to keep learning and growing.”

After KCC, Angie earned a Bachelor of Science degree in criminal justice from Illinois State University. She later completed graduate degrees in organizational leadership and business administration.

Her career has been equally wide-ranging. Angie held executive leadership positions at Olivet Nazarene University and served as area manager for the Central Division at Aqua Illinois, where she provided operational leadership across multiple communities. For more than eight years, Angie was also an adjunct business instructor at both Olivet Nazarene University and KCC.

“KCC opened doors for me that I didn’t even know existed at the time,” Angie said. “It gave me a strong foundation, lifelong skills, and the confidence to keep pursuing new opportunities.”

Her experience as a student also shaped how she viewed higher education.

“The professors truly cared about their students and wanted to see us succeed,” Angie said. “The classes challenged me, but they also gave me practical skills and experiences that I still use today.

“I’m grateful for the experiences, relationships, and lessons I gained at KCC and for the role it has played in shaping my life.”

Angie’s commitment to education is part of a broader commitment to the community. As a village trustee in Bourbonnais, she serves as chair of the Communication & Engagement Committee, where she is committed to strengthening communication between the village and its residents, supporting community events and encouraging public engagement. She also serves on the Community & Economic Development Committee and Finance Committee.

Angie also volunteers with local boards and organizations serving residents throughout Bourbonnais and Kankakee County.

An emphasis on involvement is also central to advice Angie gives students today: “Take advantage of every opportunity, ask questions, get involved, and build relationships with your instructors and classmates. KCC can be the starting point for incredible things.”
